The game

Tap. Earn. Unlock. Repeat.

An idle/incremental game built on real percussion technique. No music experience needed: the patterns teach themselves as you play.

From nailed planks to golden sticks

Start drumming on a few nailed planks with two twigs for sticks. End at 200 BPM with solid gold sticks, a concert pad and numbers that stop fitting on the screen.

The exam teacher has five faces

To reach the next phase you have to pass his exam. Miss a note and he gets annoyed, then angry, then furious. Play clean and he calms down, one face at a time.

Catch the golden notes

Golden notes fall across the pad: hover to catch them. Eight in a row climb a real major scale to the octave and triple the reward.

Jam mode

Fill the ♪ JAM sign and you get free play: the game recognises and names every rudiment you play and scores the variety.

Ghost notes

From phase 3 some notes fall see-through and faster, worth triple. The metronome turret can't see them. Only you can.

Pass. Unlock. Repeat.

Five phases and 40 rudiments, from the Single Stroke Roll to the Triple Ratamacue, each one a real percussion pattern.
