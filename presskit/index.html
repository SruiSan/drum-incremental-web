Short description

A satisfying incremental game where every click has rhythm. Tap patterns, earn Rhythm Points, unlock new techniques, and build your ultimate setup. No music experience needed — just the urge to keep going.

About the game

Drum Incremental is an idle/incremental game with a twist: instead of mindless clicking, every click has rhythm. You tap real percussion rudiments on a practice pad, earn Rhythm Points and spend them on new techniques, better gear and a Study Companion that keeps practising while you're away. Five phases, with an exam at every gate and a teacher who has five faces; golden notes that climb a real major scale; a jam mode that names every rudiment you play; and an original soundtrack that follows your tempo live. No music experience needed.

Features

  • 40 real percussion rudiments across 5 phases, from the Single Stroke Roll to the Triple Ratamacue
  • An original soundtrack by Samuel Ruiz that follows your metronome live, from 60 to 200 BPM
  • A Study Companion that keeps practising while you're away
  • 12 perks, each one a small percussion instrument: cowbell, güiro, vibraslap…
  • A metronome turret that shoots golden notes on the beat
  • Full controller support
  • 10 languages
  • 21 Steam achievements, including a secret platinum
